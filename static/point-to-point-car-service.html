Point to Point Car Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Need one ride from A to B? Noel Chauffer is a point-to-point car service — a luxury chauffeur and black car for one-way rides from one address to another, anywhere in the USA. Airport transfers, city rides, dinners, meetings, stations and long-distance trips, all in a private, chauffeured car.

How point-to-point rides work

  • Book. Fill in the booking form with your pickup and drop-off, then tap View options.
  • Flight number. For airport trips, your flight number is asked as a separate step after the form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight — and you can skip it; skipping never blocks your booking.
  • Choose your experience. Pick Business Class, Business SUV or First Class, then pay by card. Only then is a chauffeur assigned.
  • Ride. Your chauffeur drives you to your destination. After the ride ends, your invoice is sent to your phone and email.

One way, or there and back?

Point-to-point rides are one-way rides. There is no single round-trip booking — book your outward ride first, and after booking you can book your return trip as a second one-way ride. If your day has several stops, book by the hour instead and keep the same chauffeur and car with you.

Waiting time

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. All other pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply. Your chauffeur's name, phone number, car model and colour, plate number and photo are shown to you once your chauffeur is assigned, and live tracking starts once your chauffeur heads to you.

Choose your experience

Every point-to-point ride is a private, chauffeured ride in a recent high-end car. Choose the experience that fits your trip:

  • Business Class — a luxury sedan, for up to 3 passengers with luggage.
  • Business SUV — a luxury SUV, for more space and luggage.
  • First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar.

You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed — it is the class, or similar, that is guaranteed.

Cancellation

Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable. If you do not show up, the full fare applies.

Frequently asked questions

What is a point-to-point car service?

A point-to-point car service is one ride from one address to another — an airport transfer, a city ride, a dinner, a meeting, or a long-distance trip. You book the pickup and the drop-off, and your chauffeur drives you straight there.

Can I book a round trip?

Book your outward ride first; after booking you can book your return trip as a second one-way ride. There is no single round-trip booking — the return is a second one-way booking.

Can I add a stop on a point-to-point ride?

Point-to-point rides are one-way rides from one address to another. If your day has several stops, book by the hour instead and keep the same chauffeur and car with you.

How much free waiting time do I get?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. All other pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply.

Do you drive long distances?

Yes. Noel Chauffer drives anywhere in the USA — from New York to New Jersey, Connecticut, Long Island, Westchester and beyond, and longer trips too.

How do I book a point-to-point ride?

Fill in the booking form, tap View options, choose your experience, and pay by card — then your chauffeur is assigned. Bookings are currently paused while we update our prices, so please check back soon.
