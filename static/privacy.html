What we collect from customers

When you book a ride, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your pickup and drop-off addresses.
  • Your flight number, if you give one (airport trips only, and only if you choose to share it).
  • Card details you enter at booking. Card details go to our own server. Payments are currently in TEST MODE, so no real charges are made.

What we collect from drivers

When you register as a driver, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your car details and plate number.
  • The registration documents and photos you upload (driving licence, insurance, permits and profile photo), including the expiry dates you type in for them.

Location

We use your trip pickup and drop-off locations to run your booking. While a driver is heading to you, your live trip location is shown to you on the map. The driver never sees your fare or the trip distance, only their own earnings.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Run your booking and dispatch a driver to you.
  • Show trip details to you and to your driver.
  • Send invoices and trip messages.
  • Pay drivers for completed trips.

We do not sell your data to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as it is needed for bookings, driver payouts and records. Ride history is kept for at least 1 year.

Questions or deletion requests

If you have a question about this policy, or you want us to delete your information, email us at info@noelchauffer.com and we will help.
