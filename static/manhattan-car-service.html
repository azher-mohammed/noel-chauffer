Manhattan Car Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Need a car in Manhattan? Noel Chauffer is a luxury chauffeur and black car service for Manhattan — for airport transfers, point-to-point rides across the borough, and hourly hire for meetings, dinners and evenings out. Your chauffeur knows the city, plans around traffic, and waits for you: airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting, and all other pickups include 15 minutes free.

Neighbourhoods and towns we serve

Noel Chauffer drives across all of Manhattan, from Midtown to the northern tip of the island:

  • Midtown Manhattan and Times Square
  • Financial District and Lower Manhattan
  • Upper East Side and Upper West Side
  • Chelsea, SoHo, Tribeca and Greenwich Village
  • Murray Hill, Gramercy and the East Village
  • Harlem and Upper Manhattan

If your pickup or drop-off is not listed, book it anyway — Noel Chauffer drives anywhere in the USA.

Airport transfers

JFK is about 18 miles from Times Square, and the drive usually takes around 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic. LaGuardia is about 9 miles from Midtown Manhattan, around 25 to 40 minutes. Newark Airport is about 16 miles from Midtown Manhattan, around 35 to 50 minutes depending on traffic and the river crossing.

For airport trips, your flight number is asked as a separate step after the booking form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight and meet you at the right terminal, even if your flight is delayed. You can skip it and your booking still goes through. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting.

Point to point and hourly hire

Book a one-way ride from one address to another, or book by the hour and keep your chauffeur with you for as long as you need — for meetings, errands, dinners and evenings out, with as many stops as your schedule needs. For other pickups, 15 minutes of waiting is free.

Choose your experience

Every ride is a private, chauffeured ride in a recent high-end car. Choose the experience that fits your trip:

  • Business Class — a luxury sedan, for up to 3 passengers with luggage.
  • Business SUV — a luxury SUV, for more space and luggage.
  • First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar.

You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed — it is the class, or similar, that is guaranteed.

Cancellation

Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable. If you do not show up, the full fare applies.
