1. General Provisions

Noel Chauffer is a pre-booked chauffeur service across the United States. Rides are provided by independent drivers using their own cars (“Chauffeurs”). We do not own the cars. We connect you with a Chauffeur for your ride and arrange the booking, payment, and trip details through the Platform. Your use of the Services is also governed by our Privacy Policy, which explains how we collect, use, and disclose your personal information, and by the cancellation and waiting rules set out in Section 5 below and on our Cancellation and waiting page.

To book a ride, enter your pickup and drop-off addresses, date, and pickup time, choose your class, and pay by card. We offer three classes: Business Class (3 passengers, 2 bags), Business SUV (5 passengers, 5 bags), and First Class (3 passengers, 2 bags). You choose a class, not a specific car or driver.

After you pay, we send your booking to nearby Chauffeurs who match your class. The first Chauffeur to accept gets the ride. Once a Chauffeur accepts, we send you their name, phone number, car model and colour, plate number, and photo. You can follow your ride on the live map once your Chauffeur starts heading to your pickup.

There is no round-trip booking. A return journey is a separate one-way booking, made the same way as your first booking.

You are responsible for giving correct pickup and drop-off addresses, being ready at the pickup time, and, for airport pickups, giving your flight number when you book so we can follow your flight schedule.

The destination of a booking cannot be changed after the booking is made. If you need a different destination, cancel your booking and make a new one. The cancellation terms in Section 5 apply.

5. Payment, Cancellation, and Waiting Time

Payment. When you book a ride, we show you the total fare for your chosen class before you pay. You pay by card at the time of booking, before your booking is sent to Chauffeurs. Your booking is only dispatched after payment. Unpaid bookings are never sent to Chauffeurs. After payment, we email you a payment confirmation with a receipt. The fare shown to you includes the base fare and, where they apply, the airport access fee, black car fee, sales tax, congestion charges, and any toll on your route. A new customer who books through a referral link or code receives 5% off their first booking.

Hourly hire. Hourly bookings are charged per hour for the class you choose. The minimum booking is 2 hours. Each booked hour includes 25 miles. Hourly bookings can be made for up to 24 hours.

Cancellation. You may cancel a one-way ride free of charge up to 1 hour before the scheduled pickup, and an hourly booking free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled pickup. In both cases you receive a full refund. If you cancel a one-way ride less than 1 hour before pickup, or an hourly booking less than 24 hours before pickup, or if you do not show up, the full fare is kept and no refund is due.

Waiting time and no-show. Your Chauffeur waits free of charge for 60 minutes at airport pickups and for 15 minutes at all other pickups. After the free waiting time, waiting is charged at $1 per minute. If you do not show up, the full fare is kept: after 60 minutes of waiting at an airport pickup, or after 30 minutes of waiting at any other pickup.

We may change our fares at any time. The fare that applies to your ride is the fare shown to you when you book.
