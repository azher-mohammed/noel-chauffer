        Drive with Noel Chauffer

        Noel Chauffer is a luxury car-with-chauffeur service across the USA. Drivers use their own cars, see job offers in the driver app, and accept the jobs they want. The first driver to accept a job gets it.

        Why drive with Noel Chauffer?

        • Your own car, your own schedule. Go available when you want, and take the jobs you choose.
        • Earn on every trip. You see each job's pickup, drop-off and time before you accept it.
        • Weekly payouts, tracked in the driver app.
        • Referral credit for every ride you refer. Refer a driver friend and earn referral credit for each of their first 15 completed rides.
        • Jobs across New York and the USA, including airport trips.

        How to start

        1. Register in the driver app above with your documents. The app shows exactly what is needed for your city.
        2. Go available when you are ready to drive.
        3. Accept jobs and drive.

        Questions about driving with Noel Chauffer

        How do I become a driver with Noel Chauffer?

        To become a driver, register in the driver app above with your documents and your car. Registration takes minutes. After that, go available when you are ready to drive.

        How do I become a chauffeur driver in New York?

        Chauffeur jobs in New York City start the same way: register in the driver app above. These are TLC chauffeur jobs in New York — the app shows exactly which documents your city needs, and for New York City that includes your TLC License and your FH-1 insurance card.

        Are these part-time chauffeur jobs?

        Yes. These are flexible driving jobs — you go available when you want and take only the jobs you choose. There are no fixed hours and no shifts.

        Can I drive my own car?

        Yes. You drive your own car. Only cars from 2022 or newer can be registered, and your car model must be on the approved list in the app.

        What kind of driver jobs are these?

        These are black car driver jobs with a luxury chauffeur service. If you are looking for limo driver jobs or black car driver jobs in New York City, register in the driver app above and start with the jobs you want.

        Can I drive with a TLC license?

        Yes. A TLC License is one of the documents New York City drivers upload when they register in the driver app. If you are looking for TLC driver jobs, register in the driver app above.

        Which cities can I drive in?

        Noel Chauffer has chauffeur driver jobs in Los Angeles and across California, limo driver jobs in Chicago and across Illinois, and black car driver jobs in Miami and chauffeur driver jobs in Miami and across Florida — as well as chauffeur driver jobs in New York City and across the USA. Choose your state and city when you register in the driver app above, and the app shows exactly which documents your city needs.
