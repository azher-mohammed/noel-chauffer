What to expect on this ride

LaGuardia is about 9 miles from Midtown Manhattan, and the drive usually takes around 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. It is the closest of the three New York airports to Manhattan.

Your chauffeur meets you at the right terminal if your trip starts at the airport, follows your flight when you share your flight number, and drives you straight to your destination — no stops unless you ask for them. After the ride ends, your invoice is sent to your phone and email.

How your ride works

  • Book. Fill in the booking form with your pickup and drop-off, then tap View options.
  • Flight number. For airport trips, your flight number is asked as a separate step after the form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight — and you can skip it; skipping never blocks your booking.
  • Choose your experience. Pick Business Class, Business SUV or First Class, then pay by card. Only then is a chauffeur assigned.
  • Meet your chauffeur. Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall of your terminal, holding a name sign, and contacts you by phone or text after you land.
  • Ride. Your chauffeur drives you to your destination.

Waiting time and flight delays

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free 60 minutes, waiting charges apply. Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your new arrival time, so a delayed landing or a slow baggage line never becomes a worry.

Choose your experience

Every ride is a private, chauffeured ride in a recent high-end car. Choose the experience that fits your trip:

  • Business Class — a luxury sedan, for up to 3 passengers with luggage.
  • Business SUV — a luxury SUV, for more space and luggage.
  • First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar.

You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed — it is the class, or similar, that is guaranteed.

Cancellation

Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable. If you do not show up, the full fare applies.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive?

LaGuardia is about 9 miles from Midtown Manhattan, and the drive usually takes around 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. It is the closest of the three New York airports to Manhattan.

Do I need to give my flight number?

Your flight number is asked as a separate step after the booking form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight and meet you at the right terminal, even if your flight is delayed. You can skip it and your booking still goes through.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your new arrival time. The 60 minutes of free waiting covers most delays, so a slow baggage line never becomes a worry.

How much free waiting time do I get at this trip?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free 60 minutes, waiting charges apply.

Which Manhattan neighbourhoods do you drive to from LaGuardia?

Everywhere in Manhattan — Midtown, Times Square, the Financial District, the Upper East Side, the Upper West Side, Chelsea, SoHo, Tribeca, Murray Hill, Harlem and Upper Manhattan.

Which terminal is my flight at LaGuardia?

LaGuardia has three terminals — A, B and C. Terminal B serves Air Canada, American, Frontier, JetBlue, Porter, Southwest and United. Terminal C is Delta Air Lines and Delta Connection. Terminal A is the historic Marine Air Terminal. Your boarding pass shows your terminal.
