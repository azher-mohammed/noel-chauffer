JFK Terminal Pickup Guide

Last updated: 20 November 2026

JFK has five terminals, and your chauffeur meets you at the right one — inside the terminal, in the arrivals hall, with a name sign. This guide covers every JFK terminal, how the pickup works step by step, and what happens if your flight is delayed, so you can walk off the plane knowing exactly what happens next.

JFK terminals at a glance

JFK has five terminals — 1, 4, 5, 7 and 8. The free AirTrain JFK connects all terminals, so moving between them is easy. Your boarding pass shows your terminal; if you are unsure, share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur will meet you at the right one.

How pickup works, step by step

First, book your transfer: fill in the booking form with your pickup and drop-off, then tap View options. For airport trips, your flight number is asked as a separate step after the form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight and meet you at the right terminal, even if your flight is delayed — and you can skip it; skipping never blocks your booking. Then choose your experience — Business Class, Business SUV or First Class — and pay by card. Only then is a chauffeur assigned.

Once your chauffeur is assigned, you see their name, phone number, car model and colour, plate number and photo, and live tracking starts once your chauffeur heads to you. After you land, your chauffeur contacts you by phone or text. Your chauffeur waits for you inside the terminal, at arrivals, with a name sign — no searching for a car in the pickup lanes. If you prefer curbside pickup, just let your chauffeur know once you are outside with your bags.

Arrivals: what to do when you land

When you land, turn your phone on and wait for your chauffeur's message or call. Your chauffeur will already be at the terminal, following your flight. Collect your bags first — there is no rush, because airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. Then walk to the arrivals hall and look for your chauffeur holding a name sign. If you cannot find each other, call or text the number shown in your booking: your chauffeur's phone number is shown to you once your chauffeur is assigned.

If you are arriving on an international flight, allow extra time for immigration and customs — your chauffeur plans around it when you share your flight number. If you are travelling with children, or with a lot of luggage, let your chauffeur know when you book so the right car is ready: Business SUV gives more space and luggage room than Business Class.

Flight delays and waiting time

Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your new arrival time, so a delayed landing or a slow baggage line never becomes a worry. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free 60 minutes, waiting charges apply. The 60 minutes of free waiting covers most delays — and because your chauffeur is following your flight, a delay that changes your landing time is already known before you touch down.

If your flight is cancelled, or you miss your connection, contact your chauffeur as soon as you know. Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable.

Departures: getting dropped off at JFK

For departures, your chauffeur picks you up at your door and drops you at the departures level of your terminal. Share your flight number when you book so your chauffeur plans around your departure time and drops you at the right terminal. Allow plenty of time for check-in and security — for international flights, most travellers allow at least three hours, and for domestic flights at least two. Your chauffeur takes the best route for the time of day, and JFK is about 18 miles from Times Square, around 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.

Frequently asked questions

Which terminal is my flight at JFK?

JFK has five terminals — 1, 4, 5, 7 and 8. Your boarding pass shows your terminal. JetBlue flies from Terminal 5, American Airlines from Terminal 8, and Delta's international flights use Terminal 4. If you are unsure, tell us your flight number and your chauffeur will meet you at the right terminal.

Where does my chauffeur meet me at JFK?

Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall of your terminal, holding a name sign, and contacts you by phone or text after you land. If you prefer, you can also arrange a curbside pickup once you are outside with your bags.

How do I get between JFK terminals?

The free AirTrain JFK connects all terminals, so moving between them is easy. If your flight arrives at one terminal and you need another, take the AirTrain — and tell your chauffeur which terminal you will be at.
