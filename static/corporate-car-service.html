Corporate Car Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Travelling for business? Noel Chauffer is a corporate car service — a luxury chauffeur and black car service for airport transfers, meetings, roadshows and client travel across New York and anywhere in the USA. Professional chauffeurs, on-time pickups and clear communication, so your team can focus on the work, not the ride.

Book a ride

Questions? Call 929-385-7597 or email support@noelchauffer.com.

Business travel, handled

Book airport transfers for arrivals and departures, point-to-point rides between meetings, and hourly hire for roadshows and days with several stops. Your chauffeur meets you at the right terminal when you share your flight number, follows your flight, and waits for you: airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting, and all other pickups include 15 minutes free.

For teams and roadshows

For roadshows and multi-stop days, book by the hour and keep the same chauffeur and car with you for the day. For teams arriving on different flights, book one transfer per arrival and each traveller is met at the right terminal. Every ride is private and chauffeured, in a recent high-end car.

After the ride

After the ride ends, your invoice is sent to your phone and email. Your chauffeur's name, phone number, car model and colour, plate number and photo are shown to you once your chauffeur is assigned, and live tracking starts once your chauffeur heads to you.

Choose your experience

Choose the experience that fits the trip:

  • Business Class — a luxury sedan, for up to 3 passengers with luggage.
  • Business SUV — a luxury SUV, for more space and luggage.
  • First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar.

You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed — it is the class, or similar, that is guaranteed.

Cancellation

Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable. If you do not show up, the full fare applies.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do airport transfers for business travellers?

Yes. Noel Chauffer is a corporate car service for airport transfers from JFK, LaGuardia and Newark — your chauffeur meets you at the right terminal, follows your flight, and waits with 60 minutes of free waiting.

Can you handle a roadshow with several stops?

Yes. Book by the hour and keep the same chauffeur and car with you for the day, for as many stops as your schedule needs.

Can I book rides for my team?

Yes. Book one transfer per traveller or per arrival, and each traveller is met at the right terminal. Every ride is a private, chauffeured ride.

How do I get my invoice?

After the ride ends, your invoice is sent to your phone and email.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your new arrival time. The 60 minutes of free waiting covers most delays.

How do I book a corporate ride?

Fill in the booking form, tap View options, choose your experience, and pay by card — then your chauffeur is assigned. Bookings are currently paused while we update our prices, so please check back soon.
