Terminals at JFK

JFK has five terminals — 1, 4, 5, 7 and 8. The free AirTrain JFK connects all terminals, so moving between them is easy. Your boarding pass shows your terminal; if you are unsure, share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur will meet you at the right one.

TerminalWho flies from here
Terminal 1International carriers, including Air France, Lufthansa, Korean Air and others
Terminal 4Delta Air Lines international flights and many international carriers
Terminal 5JetBlue Airways
Terminal 7British Airways, Alaska Airlines and others
Terminal 8American Airlines

Terminals 2, 3 and 6 are closed or under redevelopment — if an old itinerary shows one of them, check your boarding pass for your current terminal.

How your JFK airport transfer works

  • Book. Fill in the booking form with your pickup and drop-off, then tap View options.
  • Flight number. For airport trips, your flight number is asked as a separate step after the form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight — and you can skip it; skipping never blocks your booking.
  • Choose your experience. Pick Business Class, Business SUV or First Class, then pay by card. Only then is a chauffeur assigned.
  • Meet your chauffeur. Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall of your terminal, holding a name sign, and contacts you by phone or text after you land.
  • Ride. Your chauffeur drives you to your destination. After the ride ends, your invoice is sent to your phone and email.

Meeting your chauffeur at JFK

Your chauffeur waits for you inside the terminal, at arrivals, with a name sign — no searching for a car in the pickup lanes. After you land, your chauffeur contacts you by phone or text. If you prefer curbside pickup, just let your chauffeur know once you are outside with your bags. Your chauffeur's name, phone number, car model and colour, plate number and photo are shown to you once your chauffeur is assigned, and live tracking starts once your chauffeur heads to you.

Waiting time and flight delays

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free 60 minutes, waiting charges apply. Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your new arrival time, so a delayed landing or a slow baggage line never becomes a worry.

JFK is about 18 miles from Times Square, and the drive usually takes around 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic. Noel Chauffer drives from JFK to:

Frequently asked questions about JFK airport transfers

Which terminal is my flight at JFK?

JFK has five terminals — 1, 4, 5, 7 and 8. Your boarding pass shows your terminal. JetBlue flies from Terminal 5, American Airlines from Terminal 8, and Delta's international flights use Terminal 4. If you are unsure, tell us your flight number and your chauffeur will meet you at the right terminal.

Where does my chauffeur meet me at JFK?

Your chauffeur meets you in the arrivals hall of your terminal, holding a name sign, and contacts you by phone or text after you land. If you prefer, you can also arrange a curbside pickup once you are outside with your bags.

How much free waiting time do I get at JFK?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free 60 minutes, waiting charges apply.

Do I need to give my flight number?

Your flight number is asked as a separate step after the booking form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight and meet you at the right terminal, even if your flight is delayed. You can skip it and your booking still goes through.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book and your chauffeur follows your flight and adjusts to your new arrival time. The 60 minutes of free waiting covers most delays, so a slow baggage line never becomes a worry.

How long is the drive from JFK to Manhattan?

JFK is about 18 miles from Times Square, and the drive usually takes around 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic. Your chauffeur takes the best route for the time of day.

Can I book a JFK transfer to anywhere, not just Manhattan?

Yes. Noel Chauffer drives from JFK to Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Long Island, Westchester, New Jersey, Connecticut and anywhere else in the USA. You can also book by the hour.
