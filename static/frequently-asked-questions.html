Frequently asked questions

The answers customers ask for most, before they book.

How do I book a ride?

Enter your pickup and drop-off, date and time, choose your ride, and pay by card. Your chauffeur is assigned right after payment.

When do I pay for my ride?

You pay by card on this page, right after you book, before your chauffeur is assigned.

What is the cancellation policy?

You can cancel free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, you do not get a refund.

How does waiting time work?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting; all other pickups include 15 minutes free. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply.

Do you do airport transfers?

Yes. Noel Chauffer is an airport car service for JFK, LaGuardia and Newark. Add your flight number when you book and your chauffeur will meet you at the right terminal. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting.

Can I book a round trip?

Book your outward ride first; after booking you can book your return trip as a second one-way ride.

Can I book a chauffeur by the hour?

Yes. Choose By the hour in the booking form and your chauffeur stays with you for the hours you book.

How do I get 5% off my first ride?

Book through a friend's referral link. The 5% discount applies to your first ride.

When do I see my chauffeur's details?

Once your chauffeur accepts, you see their name, phone number, car and plate, and a live map once they start heading to you. We also send the details to your phone and email.
