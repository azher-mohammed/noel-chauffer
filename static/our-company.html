Our company

Noel Chauffer is a premium chauffeur service in the USA. On-time pickups, clear communication, and a calm, comfortable ride — from booking to arrival. Every ride is prepared, so you can just step in and relax.

What we do

City-to-city rides, airport transfers, hourly hire, chauffeur hailing and limousine service — pre-booked, across the USA. We also serve businesses: corporations, travel agencies and partners.

Airport transfers

Flying in or out of JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, LAX, ORD or MIA? Your chauffeur meets you at the right terminal. Add your flight number when you book, and we adjust to your schedule.

How a ride works

Every booking works the same way: choose your class and pay by card — then your chauffeur is assigned. Once your chauffeur accepts, you see their name, phone, car and photo, with live tracking once they start heading to you.

What to expect

Professional chauffeurs — polite, experienced drivers who know the roads and respect your time. Recent high-end cars — clean, comfortable, ready for every ride. On-time pickups — your chauffeur plans the route and arrives on time, every time. A welcome at the door, help with your luggage, music and temperature set the way you like them, and phone chargers in every car.

Cancellation and waiting

Free cancellation if you cancel more than 1 hour before pickup. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of waiting free; all other pickups include 15 minutes free.

Available 24/7 — book anytime, anywhere.
