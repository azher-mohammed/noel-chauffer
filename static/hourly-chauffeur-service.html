Hourly Chauffeur Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Need a car for a few hours, not just one ride? Noel Chauffer is an hourly chauffeur service — a luxury black car and professional chauffeur by the hour, for meetings, errands, dinners, events and evenings out. Your chauffeur stays with you for the hours you book, waits while you are inside, and drives you to as many stops as your schedule needs.

Questions? Call 929-385-7597 or email support@noelchauffer.com.

How hourly hire works

  • Book. Fill in the booking form, choose By the hour, and pick how many hours you need.
  • Choose your experience. Pick Business Class, Business SUV or First Class, then pay by card. Only then is a chauffeur assigned.
  • Your chauffeur stays with you. Your chauffeur drives you from stop to stop and waits while you are inside — no re-booking, no waiting for a new car at each stop.
  • Ride. After the hours you booked end, your invoice is sent to your phone and email.

When hourly hire fits best

Hourly hire fits days with more than one stop: back-to-back meetings across Manhattan, a day of errands across the boroughs, an evening of dinners and events, a wedding day with several venues, or a shopping and sightseeing day with family. If you only need one ride from A to B, a point-to-point ride is the simpler choice.

Waiting time

All pickups other than airport pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply. Your chauffeur plans around your schedule, so a meeting that runs a little long never becomes a worry.

Choose your experience

Every hourly ride is a private, chauffeured ride in a recent high-end car. Choose the experience that fits your day:

  • Business Class — a luxury sedan, for up to 3 passengers with luggage.
  • Business SUV — a luxury SUV, for more space and luggage.
  • First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar.

You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed — it is the class, or similar, that is guaranteed.

Cancellation

Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable. If you do not show up, the full fare applies.

Frequently asked questions

How does hourly chauffeur hire work?

Choose By the hour in the booking form and pick how many hours you need. Your chauffeur stays with you for the hours you book, drives you from stop to stop, and waits while you are inside.

How many stops can I make on an hourly booking?

As many as your schedule needs, within the hours you booked. Your chauffeur drives you from stop to stop — meetings, errands, dinners, events — with no re-booking at each stop.

What is the difference between hourly and point-to-point?

Point-to-point is one ride from one address to another. Hourly hire keeps the same chauffeur and car with you for the hours you book, for as many stops as you need.

How much free waiting time do I get on an hourly booking?

All pickups other than airport pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply.

Can I extend my hours on the day?

Ask your chauffeur on the day — if the schedule allows, your chauffeur can stay longer. Your invoice after the ride reflects the hours you used.

How do I book an hourly chauffeur?

Fill in the booking form, tap View options, choose your experience, and pay by card — then your chauffeur is assigned. Bookings are currently paused while we update our prices, so please check back soon.
