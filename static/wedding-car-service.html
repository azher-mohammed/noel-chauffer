Wedding Car Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Getting married? Noel Chauffer is a wedding car service — a luxury chauffeur and black car for your wedding day, for the couple, the wedding party and guests. Arrive in a recent high-end car with a professional chauffeur, and let your chauffeur handle the driving and the timing while you enjoy the day.

Book a ride

Questions? Call 929-385-7597 or email support@noelchauffer.com.

Your wedding day, driven

Book a car for the couple, for the wedding party, or for guests travelling between venues. Your chauffeur picks you up on time, drives you to the ceremony, to photos, and to the reception — and waits while you are inside. Book by the hour for a day with several venues, or book point-to-point rides between two addresses.

For the couple and the wedding party

Choose Business Class for the couple, Business SUV for more space and luggage, or First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar — for the grand arrival. Your chauffeur's name, phone number, car model and colour, plate number and photo are shown to you once your chauffeur is assigned.

Guests and airport arrivals

Guests flying in for the wedding can book airport transfers from JFK, LaGuardia and Newark. Share the flight number when booking and the chauffeur follows the flight and meets your guest at the right terminal, with 60 minutes of free waiting.

Choose your experience

Every wedding ride is a private, chauffeured ride in a recent high-end car:

  • Business Class — a luxury sedan, for up to 3 passengers with luggage.
  • Business SUV — a luxury SUV, for more space and luggage.
  • First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar.

You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed — it is the class, or similar, that is guaranteed.

Cancellation

Cancellation is free more than 1 hour before pickup, with a full refund. If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the cancellation is not refundable. If you do not show up, the full fare applies.

Frequently asked questions

Do you drive weddings?

Yes. Noel Chauffer is a wedding car service for the couple, the wedding party and guests — for the ceremony, photos, the reception, and travel between venues.

Should I book by the hour or point to point for a wedding?

Book by the hour if your day has several venues — your chauffeur stays with you for the hours you book. Book point to point for a single ride from one address to another.

Can my guests book airport transfers for the wedding?

Yes. Guests flying in can book airport transfers from JFK, LaGuardia and Newark. Share the flight number when booking and the chauffeur follows the flight and meets your guest at the right terminal, with 60 minutes of free waiting.

Which car should we choose for the couple?

Business Class is a luxury sedan. Business SUV gives more space and luggage room. First Class — Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8, Tesla Model X, Bentley Bentayga, Bentley Flying Spur, Bentley Mulsanne, Mercedes-Benz Maybach S-Class, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Rolls-Royce Ghost, Rolls-Royce Phantom or Lamborghini Urus, or similar — is the grandest arrival. You choose a class, and the exact model is never guaranteed.

How much free waiting time do we get?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. All other pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply.

How do I book a wedding car?

Fill in the booking form, tap View options, choose your experience, and pay by card — then your chauffeur is assigned. Bookings are currently paused while we update our prices, so please check back soon.
