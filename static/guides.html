Frequently asked questions

What do the Noel Chauffer guides cover?

The guides answer the questions people ask before they book: which New York airport to fly into, how JFK terminal pickup works, how a black car service compares with an on-demand app, and what affects the cost of a black car in New York City.

Which airport is closest to Manhattan?

LaGuardia is the closest of the three New York airports to Manhattan — about 9 miles from Midtown Manhattan, around 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. JFK is about 18 miles from Times Square, and Newark Airport is about 16 miles from Midtown Manhattan.

How much free waiting time do I get at the airport?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting. All other pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting. After the free waiting time, waiting charges apply.

Do I need to give my flight number?

Your flight number is asked as a separate step after the booking form. It helps your chauffeur follow your flight and meet you at the right terminal, even if your flight is delayed. You can skip it and your booking still goes through.
