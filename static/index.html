      Cancellation & waiting

      Simple rules, stated plainly before you book.

      Payment

      • After you enter your trip, choose your class — Business Class or Business SUV. Each class shows its own single total.
      • You always see one single total before you pay — tax included, no breakdown, no hidden charges.
      • You pay by card on this page, right after you book.
      • Your chauffeur is assigned right after payment.
      • If you cancel more than 1 hour before pickup, your payment is refunded in full.

      Cancellation

      • Free cancellation if you cancel more than 1 hour before pickup.
      • If you cancel 1 hour or less before pickup, the full price of the ride applies.

      Waiting time

      • Airport pickups: 60 minutes of waiting free.
      • All other pickups: 15 minutes of waiting free.
      • After the free waiting time, waiting is charged at $1/min.

      No-show

      • If you do not show up, the full price of the ride applies.
      • Your chauffeur waits 60 minutes at airports and 30 minutes at other pickups before a ride is marked as a no-show.
